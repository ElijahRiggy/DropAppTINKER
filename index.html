    How ordering works

    1. You order

      Pick what you like and check out. You get a confirmation email straight away.

    2. The maker packs it

      We pass your order to the workshop that makes it, and they pack it within 2 business days.

    3. It ships to your door

      Delivery takes 7 to 14 business days in Canada and the US, with tracking sent by email.

    Questions

    Something else? Email

    Why does delivery take one to two weeks?

    We don't keep a warehouse. Each item ships directly from the workshop that makes it, which keeps prices lower but adds a few days in transit.

    Can I return something?

    Yes. You have 30 days from delivery to send back anything unopened for a full refund. If it arrives damaged, email us a photo and we'll replace it.

    Are these for kids or adults?

    Both. Most of the bench is aimed at teens and adults, and every product lists a recommended age.

    Do you ship outside Canada and the US?

    Not yet. Email us where you are and we'll let you know when we do.
